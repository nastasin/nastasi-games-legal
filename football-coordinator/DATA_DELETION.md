# Data Deletion

**Football Coordinator**, published by **Nastasi LLC**.

Football Coordinator does not require you to create an account, and we do not store your personal data on our own servers. Your game data — settings and records of unlocked in-app purchases — is stored **locally on your device**.

## How to delete your data

- **Uninstall the app.** Uninstalling Football Coordinator removes all game data stored on your device, including settings, saved progress, and records of unlocked purchases.
- **In-app purchases** are recorded by Google Play under your Google account. You can review your purchase history in the Google Play Store app.

## Requesting deletion of other data

If you would like us to delete any other data we may control that is associated with you, email **nastasi65@gmail.com** with your request. We will respond and delete such data where applicable.

## Third-party services

We use Google Play (billing) and Unity Gaming Services, which process limited technical and diagnostic data under their own policies. See our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) for details.
