# Privacy Policy

For **Football Coordinator** ("the App"), published by **Nastasi LLC** ("we", "us").

**Effective date:** 2026-10-17

This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and who it is shared with. By using the App you agree to this policy.

## 1. Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide your name. The App and the third-party services it uses may process the following:

- **Purchase information** — when you buy an in-app product, the platform's billing service (Google Play Billing on Android, or Apple's App Store / StoreKit on iOS) processes the transaction and provides us a purchase token/receipt so we can grant the item. We do *not* receive your full payment card details.
- **Device & usage information** — technical data such as device model, operating-system version, language, and app-interaction/diagnostic events, used to operate the game and improve stability.
- **Advertising identifier & ad-related data** — if and when the App shows ads (see Section 4), the advertising SDKs it includes may process your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android; Apple's IDFA on iOS, only with your permission — see Section 5), IP address, coarse location derived from IP, and ad-interaction data, to select and measure ads.

## 2. How we use information

- To provide and restore in-app purchases and unlocked content.
- To operate, maintain, and improve the game and fix problems.
- To display and measure advertising, when ads are enabled (see Section 4).

## 3. Third-party services

The App uses the following services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

- **Google Play Billing** (Android in-app purchases) — <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- **Apple App Store / StoreKit** (iOS in-app purchases) — <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>
- **Unity Gaming Services / Unity Technologies** (game services, diagnostics) — <https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy>
- **Unity LevelPlay / ironSource** (ad mediation) — <https://www.is.com/privacy-policy/>
- **Unity Ads** (advertising) — <https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy>

Ad mediation may route ad requests to additional advertising networks, each subject to its own privacy policy.

## 4. Advertising

The App includes ad-mediation SDKs (Unity LevelPlay / ironSource and Unity Ads). When advertising is enabled, these SDKs and their partner networks may collect and use the data described in Section 1 to serve and measure ads, including limited profiling for ad relevance. If you purchase the "Remove Ads" product, ads are disabled for you.

## 5. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS, if the App or its ad partners would use your IDFA to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies, the App will request your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, tracking identifiers are not used for that purpose; you may still see non-personalized ads.

## 6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Advertising, where shown, is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 7. Data sharing & sale

We do not sell your personal information for money. Sharing of advertising identifiers with ad partners for personalized advertising may, in some jurisdictions (e.g., California), be considered a "sale" or "sharing"; where required, we honor opt-out rights and platform-level ad-personalization and tracking settings. Otherwise, data is shared only with the service providers listed above to operate the App.

## 8. Data retention

Purchase entitlements are stored on your device and, where applicable, by the platform billing service so purchases can be restored. Diagnostic and advertising data is retained by the relevant provider per its own policy.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on your region (e.g., EEA/UK GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data, to opt out of the sale/sharing of personal information or of personalized advertising, and to object to certain processing. To make a request, contact us at the address below. You can also control ad personalization through your device settings (Android: "Ads" settings / reset advertising ID; iOS: App Tracking Transparency and "Personalized Ads" settings).

## 10. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by a new effective date on this page.
