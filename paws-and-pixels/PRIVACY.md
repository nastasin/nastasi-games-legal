# Privacy Policy

For **Paws & Pixels** ("the App"), published by **Nastasi LLC** ("we", "us").

**Effective date:** 2026-10-17

This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and who it is shared with. By using the App you agree to this policy.

## 1. Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide your name. The App and the third-party services it uses may process the following:

- **Purchase information** — when you buy an in-app product (such as a coin pack), Google Play processes the transaction and provides us a purchase token/receipt so we can grant the item. We do *not* receive your full payment card details.
- **Device & usage information** — technical data such as device model, operating-system version, language, and app-interaction/diagnostic events, used to operate the game and improve stability.
- **Advertising identifier & ad-related data** — because the App shows ads (see Section 4), the advertising SDKs it includes may process your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID), IP address, coarse location derived from IP, and ad-interaction data, to select and measure ads.

Your game progress (solved puzzles, coin balance, settings, and your ad-personalization choice) is stored **locally on your device**; we do not collect it on our own servers.

## 2. How we use information

- To provide in-app purchases and granted content.
- To operate, maintain, and improve the game and fix problems.
- To display and measure advertising (see Section 4).

## 3. Third-party services

The App uses the following services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

- **Google Play Billing** (in-app purchases) — <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- **Unity Gaming Services / Unity Technologies** (game services, diagnostics) — <https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy>
- **Unity LevelPlay / ironSource** (ad mediation) — <https://www.is.com/privacy-policy/>
- **Unity Ads** (advertising) — <https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy>

Ad mediation may route ad requests to additional advertising networks, each subject to its own privacy policy.

## 4. Advertising

The App includes ad-mediation SDKs (Unity LevelPlay / ironSource and Unity Ads), including rewarded ads you may choose to watch in exchange for in-game rewards. These SDKs and their partner networks may collect and use the data described in Section 1 to serve and measure ads, including limited profiling for ad relevance. When you first launch the App, we ask whether you consent to the use of your data for **personalized advertising**. If you decline, ads are still shown but are **non-personalized**, and we signal the ad networks not to sell or share your personal information. You can change this choice at any time on the in-app **Privacy** screen (see Section 7).

## 5. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Advertising, where shown, is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 6. Data sharing & sale

We do not sell your personal information for money. Sharing of advertising identifiers with ad partners for personalized advertising may, in some jurisdictions (e.g., California), be considered a "sale" or "sharing"; when you decline personalized ads, we signal an opt-out, and we honor platform-level ad-personalization settings. Otherwise, data is shared only with the service providers listed above to operate the App.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on your region (e.g., EEA/UK GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data, to opt out of the sale/sharing of personal information or of personalized advertising, and to object to certain processing. You can change your ad-personalization choice at any time on the in-app **Privacy** screen. To make any other request, contact us at the address below. You can also control ad personalization through your device settings (Android: "Ads" settings / reset advertising ID).

## 8. Data retention

Purchase entitlements are stored on your device and, where applicable, by Google Play so purchases can be verified. Diagnostic and advertising data is retained by the relevant provider per its own policy.

## 9. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by a new effective date on this page.
