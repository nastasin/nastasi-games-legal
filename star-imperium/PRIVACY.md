# Privacy Policy

For **Star Imperium** ("the Game"), published by **Nastasi LLC** ("we", "us").

**Effective date:** September 1, 2026

What information the Game handles, and how. By playing the Game, you agree to this Policy.

> **In short**
> - Your game progress and settings are saved **locally on your device**, not sent to us.
> - If you play **online multiplayer or trading**, the Game connects to a game server and exchanges a player identifier and the match/trade data needed to run those features.
> - The Game writes **local crash/error logs** on your device to help diagnose problems; they are not automatically transmitted to us.
> - We do **not** sell your personal information.

## 1. Summary

Your game progress and settings are saved locally on your device, not sent to us. If you play online multiplayer or trading, the Game connects to a game server and exchanges a player identifier and the match/trade data needed to run those features. The Game writes local crash/error logs on your device to help diagnose problems; they are not automatically transmitted to us. We do not sell your personal information.

## 2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The Game stores the following in your operating system's user-data folder, on your device only:

- **Game progress:** decks, card inventory, in-game credits, campaign progress, faction selection, daily-reward status, and tutorial progress.
- **Settings:** audio, video/display, graphics-quality, and resolution preferences.
- **Crash/error logs:** a local log file recording errors, to help diagnose crashes.

This data stays on your device. You can delete it by clearing the Game's saved data or uninstalling the Game.

## 3. Information Processed for Online Features

If you choose to use online multiplayer or card trading, we (or our hosting provider) process the following to operate those features:

- a **player/session identifier** used to connect you to matches and to your opponents or trade partners;
- **gameplay and trade data** exchanged during a session (for example, moves, match state, and offered cards);
- basic **connection data** such as IP address, which is inherent to networked play and used for connectivity, security, and abuse prevention.

We use this information only to provide, secure, and maintain online features. We do not use it for advertising. We do not operate our own game servers; online connections are handled through Steam, and any server-side connection data is retained by Valve Corporation under its own practices (see Section 6).

## 4. Third-Party Platforms and Services

- **Distribution platform (e.g., Steam / Valve Corporation):** if you obtain or run the Game through a third-party store, that platform collects and processes information under its own privacy policy, which we do not control. See the platform's policy (for Steam: Valve's Privacy Policy).
- **Hosting/networking provider:** online features use Steam's networking services, provided by Valve Corporation, which processes connection data to deliver those features under Valve's own privacy policy.
- The Game does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

## 5. How We Use Information

We use the limited information described above to: run and maintain the Game and its online features; save and restore your progress and settings on your device; diagnose and fix crashes and bugs; and protect against cheating, fraud, and abuse.

## 6. Data Retention

Local data remains on your device until you delete it. We do not run our own game servers and do not retain server-side session or connection data ourselves; any such data for online play is handled and retained by Valve Corporation (Steam) under Valve's own privacy policy and retention practices.

## 7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed for online features. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game's online features, contact us at the address below and we will take appropriate steps.

## 9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information, or to object to it (for example, under the EU/UK GDPR or California's CCPA/CPRA). Because most data is stored locally on your device, you can exercise many of these rights directly by managing or deleting the Game's saved data. For requests about any server-side data, contact us at the address below.

## 10. International Transfers

If you use online features, information may be processed in the United States and other regions where Valve Corporation (Steam) operates its networking infrastructure. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.
